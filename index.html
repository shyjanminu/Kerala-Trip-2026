📜 Pilgrimage Guidelines (Do's & Don'ts)
DO'S (Follow)
  • Assemble at Church campus 30 mins before departure.
  • Wear Catechism ID card & maintain reverence at holy shrines.
  • Stay with your assigned group leader at every stop.
  • Keep the luxury coach clean and tidy throughout.
DON'TS (Avoid)
  • Do not leave the bus at any stop without teacher's permission.
  • Do not stand or move along the aisle while coach is in motion.
  • Avoid carrying expensive jewelry or heavy cash.
  • Do not delay bus restart timing at tea or meal breaks.
